Every stone has its own pace

The status follows the last recorded location, starting with birth. A new find brings a stone back to Alive.

Alive0–5 days · A fresh hello! Someone has recorded this stone recently.
On a journey6–14 days · Out on an adventure, waiting for its next hello.
Quiet15–29 days · It has been quiet for a while. Perhaps someone will find it soon.
Lost30+ days · No recent news. It can still return whenever someone finds it.
DemoA sample story to explore. Demo stones keep this label and do not count as alive.
